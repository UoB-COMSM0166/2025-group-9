## Week 9 – Exploring the most suitable design approach for our game.

## The early design phase was divided between two primary art styles:-
1. Pixelated Design
2. Non-Pixelated (Flat/Modern) Design

This dual-approach strategy enabled the team to evaluate and compare the strengths of each visual style in terms of 
aesthetics, coherence,and compatibility with gameplay mechanics.
1. The pixel art designs emphasized a retro, nostalgic appearance that evokes familiarity and simplicity.
2. The non-pixelated (flat-design) alternatives leaned toward a cleaner, more modern, and educational interface, which could be more suitable for informational or simulation-based experiences.

Each design direction was developed by different team members to ensure diversity and creativity. This parallel exploration phase allowed for dynamic comparisonand constructive feedback across visual formats.

 ## Homepage Design Exploration
As part of the early design process, we also developed two versions of the homepage (title screen) to test which style aligned better 
with the rest of the game:
1. A pixelated version using blocky characters and pixel-based assets that matched the retro aesthetic of the in-game visuals.
2. A non-pixelated version (flat/modern design) featuring smoother, vector-like elements and icons, with a cleaner interface suited for educational content.
   These homepage concepts were included in the comparison process alongside the level and environment designs.
   The team evaluated both versions and ultimately chose the pixelated homepage to maintain visual consistency throughout the game.
---
The design development process was a critical early step in shaping the overall identity and user experience of the game. By exploring multiple visual styles and encouraging contributions from all team members, we were able to assess the impact of each approach
in the context of gameplay. Ultimately, the decision to adopt the pixelated design was grounded in both aesthetic preference and
functional alignment with the game's mechanics and genre. This unified visual direction now serves as a strong foundation
for the rest of the game's development, ensuring consistency, clarity, and an engaging player experience moving forward.
